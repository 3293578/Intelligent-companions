Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: September 16, 2026 · Wyth / thewyth.com

Wyth is currently free and does not accept subscription payments. There is no Wyth subscription to cancel or purchase price to refund.

1. Model-provider charges

You supply your own model-provider account and API key. Any charges from that provider are between you and the provider and are governed by its billing, cancellation, and refund rules. Wyth cannot issue refunds for third-party model usage.

2. Ending use

You may stop using Wyth at any time. Reload or close the browser tab to clear the in-memory model key, and use your browser controls to clear locally stored profiles and chat history.

3. Contact

For product help or questions about this policy, email support@thewyth.com.
